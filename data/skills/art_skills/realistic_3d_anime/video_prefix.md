# 视频渲染目标 · 参考优先的半写实三维

## Character Reference

Each character uses one complete reference sheet in one Picture slot. Face close-up, front full body, side full body and back full body panels, when visible, show the same person, never separate characters. Preserve the same facial proportions, including the observed eye-to-face size, nose structure and jaw shape, hairstyle, body proportions, clothing structure and accessories throughout motion. Do not copy the sheet layout, repeated figures or display background into the video. Changes require an explicit timed story transition; rendering style must not override identity.

## Environment Reference

The provided environment image defines the location: preserve its architecture, spatial layout, background landmarks, lighting direction and atmosphere. Do not substitute a generic scene. Weather and illumination change only as required by the story; maintain coherent lighting on characters and surroundings.

## Video Style

Cinematic semi-realistic 3D rendering at the current character references' observed level of realism and stylization. Use the provided references as the visual foundation. Preserve:

- character identity
- facial structure
- hairstyle and hair rendering
- clothing design
- material response
- lighting direction
- environment continuity

Characters and surroundings share coherent lighting and contact shadows. Do not turn material refinement into new facial anatomy, a different outfit or a live-action actor.

## Action, Camera, Sound

Prioritize character consistency, physical motion, camera movement, then environmental detail. Describe actions with clear physical cause and effect. Maintain:

- body momentum
- gravity
- contact points
- character reactions
- camera continuity
- temporal consistency

Describe observable actions and results in short sentences, with one main action and one main camera path at a time. Preserve who acts on whom; avoid long nested cause-and-effect clauses and invented secondary events. Hair, cloth and water respond naturally to the main motion. Establish framing using wide establishing shot, medium interaction shot, close facial shot, tracking shot or high angle shot as appropriate. Add synchronized physical sounds and only supplied speech.

For dialogue use:

<Subject N> says:
<d>[Language] dialogue text.</d>

Do not put language labels such as English or Chinese inside the spoken dialogue content.

Describe characters and environments where they first affect the video, keeping every reference identity and role unambiguous. Place the style opening before the timed action, then describe action, camera and synchronized sound in the clearest cinematic order. Do not require fixed section names, a fixed section count or a fixed section order.
